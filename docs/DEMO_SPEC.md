## Judge click-path
1. Open app → North Indian Ocean. 2. **Pick the surface data source** — 🛰️ real satellite
observations or 🧊 GLORYS reanalysis. 3. Pick location + date. 4. **Reconstruct** →
5. vertical temperature profile with uncertainty band. 6. Nearest independent Argo overlay + error.
7. Anomaly toggle. 8. Observation-priority map. 9. "Why this matters".

## LIVE vs CACHED
`artifacts/demo_scenes.json` holds scenes **generated by the real model** and stamped with the
checkpoint, provenance and timestamp that produced them. If the UI serves one it MUST be labelled
**CACHED (VERIFIED)** — never presented as live inference. Nothing in the demo is hand-written.

## The two numbers — do not conflate
| number | meaning |
|---|---|
| **+0.626** | skill vs climatology on the GLORYS 2022 holdout — *same source as training, easier* |
| **+0.395** | skill vs climatology vs **independent Argo** — *different instrument, the real number* |

**Quote +0.395.** Satellite-driven RMSE **0.951 °C** vs climatology **1.573 °C**, on 879 Argo
profiles. Domain-shift cost vs reanalysis inputs: **−0.7 %** (satellite is marginally better).

## THE SCENE TO SHOW — and what it admits
`bay-of-bengal-post-monsoon` vs an independent float **38.6 km away, 2 days apart**:

```
  depth   model   sigma    ARGO    error
      5   28.94    0.11   28.87   +0.07
     50   28.72    0.17   28.75   -0.04
    150   18.92    0.26   20.93   -2.01   <-- thermocline
    200   15.35    0.17   17.38   -2.03   <-- thermocline
    500   10.22    0.13   10.13   +0.09
   1000    6.83    0.13    6.82   +0.01   <-- 0.01 degC at 1 km depth
  RMSE 0.894 degC over 14 levels
```

Two honest halves, and we lead with the second:
- **Strong:** 0.01 °C at 1000 m, sub-0.3 °C through the surface layer, from satellite data alone.
- **Weak, and we say it first:** −2.0 °C at 150–200 m while the model claims **±0.26 °C**. The
  uncertainty is badly **overconfident exactly where the error is largest** — a live instance of
  `DECISIONS.md` D-016, now confirmed on real data rather than fixtures.

**Say this out loud in the demo.** The error concentrates at the thermocline, which is precisely
where per-depth skill is worst (+0.507 at 100 m) and where interannual variability peaks
(σ 1.167 °C at 100 m). Three independent measurements agreeing on where the problem is — that is a
diagnosis, not an excuse. What we must NOT do is quote the MC-dropout band as a confidence interval
until Unit A re-calibrates it.

## WHAT TO COPY TO THE DEMO LAPTOP  [VERIFIED by fresh-clone test]
The app reads `data/processed/`, **not** `data/raw/`. Copying the raw NetCDF is 2.9 GB of waste.

| copy | size | why |
|---|---|---|
| `artifacts/` | 125 MB | model, climatology, Argo, provenance |
| `data/processed/` | **35 MB** | `grids.npz` + `satellite_grids.npz` — what `reconstruct()` actually opens |
| `data/raw/` | 2.9 GB | **NOT needed** — only for rebuilding from scratch |

Total ~160 MB. [VERIFIED] With `artifacts/` but no `data/`, provenance still reads `real-glorys`
and the app serves cleanly with a clear error — it fails safe, but it cannot reconstruct. Add the
35 MB and both sources work.
